Rigetti Computing

Platforms & Vendors

Rigetti is a full-stack superconducting company operating its own fabrication facility, Fab-1 in California, and selling cloud access to its Aspen and Ankaa systems. Its Ankaa-3 processor carries 84 qubits with a modular design intended to tile into larger machines. Rigetti has also demonstrated entanglement across separate chips as a scale-up path.

Key factRigetti's ownership of Fab-1 made it one of the few quantum companies controlling its own chip fabrication end to end.
Leader lensRigetti's fab ownership is its differentiator and its cost burden. Government and research buyers value it; enterprise buyers should test the software stack depth.
